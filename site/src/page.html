Why fyrr

Without asking the whole company.

  1. Find01

    Works in the AI you already usePlug fyrr into Claude, Cursor or any MCP app. Ask there, and it searches your docs, chats, tickets and code.

  2. Protect02

    Only what each person may readIt follows each document’s access rules, taken from the app or set by you. Nobody finds what they could not open themselves.

  3. Scale03

    As many readers as you needReaders only read from the bucket and never coordinate, so you add more as you grow. No database or search cluster to outgrow.

  4. Own04

    Your data stays yoursIt runs in your own cloud. Text leaves it only for the AI model you choose to understand it.

Also indexes local files. A Python SDK lets you write your own connector.
